---
name: broll-reel
description: Copies a reference reel onto the user's own b-roll. Reads the reel's cut times, its on-screen words (place, size, colour) and its sound, picks one clip from the user's b-roll folders (Google Drive or local, broll.py) for every shot, rebuilds the reel at 9:16 with the same words over the new clips and the reel's audio, renders it and opens it in the editor so the user changes anything with notes. No talking head, no script. Use when a home page job says `mode=broll-reel`, or the user says 'copy this reel with my b-roll', 'make this reel with my clips', 'same text, my b-roll', 'redo this reel with my footage'. Not for editing the user's own talking take (that is start, cut and style-edit) and not for measuring a creator's style (that is style-match).
license: MIT
