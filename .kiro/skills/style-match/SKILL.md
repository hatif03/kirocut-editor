---
name: style-match
description: Reverse-engineers a short-form creator's video format from their real numbers. Pulls a TikTok or YouTube Shorts profile (or pasted links, Instagram reels too) with view, like and save counts, transcribes the top videos word for word, measures pace, pauses and fillers, maps each video into timed beats, and writes a teardown.md someone can film from, a teardown.html page of their graphics, motion and sound, and the style.json the kirocut plugin edits from (on its own it can write an edit plan as a table and .srt). Use when the user wants to analyse, break down or study a creator ('analyse @handle', 'tear down @handle', 'teardown of @handle', 'break down this creator', 'what makes this creator's videos work'), pastes TikTok, YouTube or Instagram links and asks what the pattern is, or asks to set up style-match. Not for editing the user's own video like a creator when kirocut is installed (its start skill), not for researching a topic, and not for cutting raw footage.
