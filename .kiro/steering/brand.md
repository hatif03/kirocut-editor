# Brand

- Name: **KiroCut**. One word, capital K, capital C.
- Pitch: **Turn your Kiro IDE into a video editor.**
- Mark: `docs/logo.svg`. Kiro's ghost (white, two eyes) on Kiro purple (`#7B5CFF`), crossed by a black film strip whose sprocket holes show the purple through. The home page, review rail, and favicon use this same mark: edit `docs/logo.svg`, then `py tests/logo_sync.py` (check_plugins fails when a copy differs).
- Style matching is part of KiroCut. Call it "Match a style" in the window. It measures videos the person pastes. It is not a page of other creators' accounts.
- Do not add example Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube URLs for real people.
