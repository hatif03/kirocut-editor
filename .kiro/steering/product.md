# Product

KiroCut turns the Kiro IDE into a video editor.

Open this folder in Kiro and say "edit my video". Kiro cuts a talking-head take (retakes, false starts, pauses), then styles it with captions, zooms, cards and sound. The person approves the cut, the look, and the render. The editor window is a local page. Kiro, in this workspace, does the work.

## Who it is for

Someone who already works in Kiro and wants that same window to edit their own footage. They are not asked to learn a separate editor brand.

## What it does

- **Cut.** Remove mistakes from the speaker's own take.
- **Match a style.** The person pastes 3 to 6 videos edited the same way, or builds the look themselves. KiroCut measures cut pace, zooms, captions, cards and sound, saves that as a style, and edits their video in it. The references are ones they choose. This repo does not ship example accounts or links to other creators.
- **Shorts.** Find clips in a long video or podcast and edit each one.
- **Product video.** A website URL in, a launch film out.
- **Taste.** A correction ("captions too small") is saved and used on the next video.

## Voice

The product name is KiroCut. The pitch is "turn your Kiro IDE into a video editor". User-facing copy uses that name and that pitch. Style matching stays a feature and is described as measuring reference videos the person supplies.

## Out of scope for the brand

Do not add sample Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube links that point at other people's accounts. Do not present KiroCut as a gallery of other creators.
